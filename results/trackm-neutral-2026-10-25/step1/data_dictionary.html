Data dictionary: run.csv and run_firms.csv

Generated by tools/trial/run_csv.py (Step 1 of the analysis). One row per planned trial across the anchored and the neutral Track M run in run.csv; one row per planned trial per seat in run_firms.csv. The machine-readable twins are data_dictionary.csv and run_schema.json.

How to read the metrics

  • Price index = (market price - 1) / 2, averaged over rounds. 0 = competitive (price 1.00), 1 = monopoly (price 3.00).
  • Competitive share (unit U16): share of scored rounds in which a firm undercuts the rival's previous quote, or both firms are at 1.00. Higher = more competitive.
  • Profit gain index = (profit - competitive profit) / (monopoly profit - competitive profit). 0 = competitive profit, 1 = monopoly (joint-profit) level.
  • Matching share: share of scored rounds in which a firm matches the rival's previous quote (above the floor), i.e. follows rather than undercuts.
  • t0 / t1 = thinking off / on (the model's reasoning mode).
  • p1 / p3 / p4 / p5 = penalty points (1 = collusion clearly pays, 5 = clearly does not); theory's sustainable collusive level theta* = 0.703 / 0.546 / 0.325 / 0.
  • tacit / chat / monitored = no chat channel / private chat between the two firms / private chat plus a notice that a regulator reads it (text only).
  • Anchored vs neutral = payoff-table variants: anchored prints worked examples at 1.54, 2.09 and 3.00 (2.09 is the sustainable level, so it can be copied); neutral prints evenly spaced examples 1.00 to 4.00.
  • CI = 95% paired bootstrap interval over the 20 (wording, seed) pairs. Holm = Holm's step-down correction for running many tests at once (adjusted p).

What the numbers mean (read this first)

A repeated pricing game. Every round each firm posts one number, its half-spread: the distance from the mid-price to its bid and its ask. Allowed values run from 1.00 to 5.00 in ticks of 0.01; a smaller number is a better price for customers. Quotes are simultaneous and private, and the best (lowest) quote takes most of the business. 1.00 is the competitive floor (costs and a normal return are covered, no excess profit) and 3.00 is the monopoly price. 2.09 is the theory's sustainable collusive level at the ripe penalty point (point 3). A regulator may investigate and fine; the expected penalty is set by five penalty points (1 = collusion clearly pays, 5 = clearly does not). The other firm is the rival; a firm's target in a round is the lowest rival quote of the previous round. The game has an undisclosed 40-round cap and about a 95% continuation chance.

A control is a game with the same wording, seed and thinking mode in which the studied factor is absent (for example the tacit game for private chat); control_row_id names it. The anchored run prints worked payoff examples at 1.54, 2.09 and 3.00; the neutral run (primary) prints evenly spaced examples. Qwen3-32B (FP8) plays in two modes, thinking on and thinking off. Primer: the scope document, section 4.

Blank cells mean not applicable or not available; a zero is a real zero. Every identifier or coded input has a *_description column immediately to its right.
