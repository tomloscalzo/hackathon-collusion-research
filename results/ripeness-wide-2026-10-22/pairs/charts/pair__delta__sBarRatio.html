Interpretation

Whether firms collude: It appears that collusion peaks at middle values of the reservation spread ratio (the room between cost and the spread at which customers stop trading; "room" below), and rises then falls with patience (the chance the market continues another round). This seems to be the sum of opposing effects. In markets with no minimum price step, more room means the same degree of collusion produces a wider spread that the regulator's screen (its rule for opening investigations when spreads are wide) notices more, so collusion falls; in markets with a minimum price step, a narrow room leaves few or no price steps above normal competition, so collusion rises with room. Patience lifts collusion at every reservation spread ratio, and the dip at the most patient end appears to be a modelling simplification in markets with a minimum price step (an investigation that finds nothing is treated as worse for firms than a conviction).

How hard they collude: It appears that collusion intensity (how far the chosen spread sits between the competitive and the monopoly level) peaks at a fairly low, but not the lowest, reservation spread ratio (the room between cost and the spread at which customers stop trading; "room" below), and rises then falls with patience (the chance the market continues another round). In markets with no minimum price step, intensity falls steadily as the room widens, most likely because the same degree of collusion is a wider spread that the regulator's screen (its rule for opening investigations when spreads are wide) notices more. The narrowest room is pulled down by markets with a minimum price step, where a large step leaves no space to collude and a smaller step offers only an all-or-nothing jump to the full-collusion price.

Details
Share where collusion is chosen (left chart)

The share of cells where collusion is chosen peaks at patience = 0.9 (0.37) and is lower at both ends (0.17 at 0.5, 0.27 at 0.99). Why: patience helps up to a point (future cartel profits are worth protecting and the price-war threat bites); the decline at very high patience comes from the π_post = 0 calibration in tick markets (see Analysis).

The share of cells where collusion is chosen peaks at the reservation spread ratio = 5 (0.33) and is lower at both ends (0.24 at 2, 0.28 at 12). Why: more room between cost and the customers' walk-away spread means bigger cartel profits, but the screen reacts to the absolute markup, so the wide spreads a cartel would charge in roomy markets are also more likely to be flagged.

Highest: 0.41 at δ = 0.9, s̄/s₀ = 5; lowest: 0.11 at δ = 0.5, s̄/s₀ = 2.

Caveat: patterns involving tick, patience or P(evidence) are partly shaped by the π_post = 0 calibration in tick markets (a recorded limitation).

Mechanism detail: Two effects of a wider demand range s̄/s₀ are averaged here. In tick-free markets s̄/s₀ acts only as a multiplier on the screen (the monopoly markup is (s̄/s₀ − 1)/2 in absolute units and the screen measures absolute markup), so the share falls monotonically (δ = 0.99: 0.642 → 0.390). In tick markets a narrow range leaves few or no tick steps above the Nash margin (with a 0.5 tick and s̄/s₀ = 2 the collusive range is empty), so the tick share rises from 0.064 to 0.184 at δ = 0.99. The inverted U peaking at s̄/s₀ = 5 (0.408 at δ = 0.9) is the sum of the two; patience lifts every column through the incentive constraint, and the δ = 0.99 dip again comes from tick cells.

Chosen collusion intensity θ* (right chart)

The mean collusion intensity θ* peaks at patience = 0.9 (0.26) and is lower at both ends (0.10 at 0.5, 0.17 at 0.99). Why: patience helps up to a point (future cartel profits are worth protecting and the price-war threat bites), but very patient coalitions choose lower margins to avoid the screen, and in tick markets the π_post = 0 calibration makes long horizons costly.

The mean collusion intensity θ* peaks at the reservation spread ratio = 3 (0.25) and is lower at both ends (0.20 at 2, 0.16 at 12). Why: more room between cost and the customers' walk-away spread means bigger cartel profits, but the screen reacts to the absolute markup, so the wide spreads a cartel would charge in roomy markets are also more likely to be flagged.

Highest: 0.32 at δ = 0.9, s̄/s₀ = 3; lowest: 0.07 at δ = 0.5, s̄/s₀ = 12.

Caveat: patterns involving tick, patience or P(evidence) are partly shaped by the π_post = 0 calibration in tick markets (a recorded limitation).

Mechanism detail: θ* peaks at s̄/s₀ = 3 in every row (e.g. 0.317 at δ = 0.9). Tick-free θ* falls monotonically with s̄/s₀ (δ = 0.9: 0.480 → 0.220) because the same θ is a larger absolute markup and therefore a higher screen hazard. At s̄/s₀ = 2, 0.5-tick cells cannot collude at all and 0.25-tick cells have a single step (θ* is 0 or 1), which pulls that column down.
