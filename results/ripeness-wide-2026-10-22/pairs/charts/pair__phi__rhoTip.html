Interpretation

Whether firms collude: It appears that collusion falls sharply as the tip probability (the chance each round that an investigation starts from a tip, whatever the price) rises and rises with the preferenced volume share (the share of orders split evenly between firms whatever their price), by a similar amount at every tip probability. Tips seem to work by shortening the cartel's expected life, which also makes cheating more tempting, and the preferenced volume share acts as a counterweight by making undercutting less rewarding. Their effects therefore look nearly additive.

How hard they collude: It appears that collusion intensity (how far the chosen spread sits between the competitive and the monopoly level) falls as the tip probability (the chance each round that an investigation starts from a tip, whatever the price) rises at every preferenced volume share (the share of orders split evenly between firms whatever their price), and that the preferenced volume share adds a similar amount at every tip probability. The fall with tips most likely comes from fewer cartels forming, even though the cartels that do form tend to price higher when a whistleblower might expose them anyway.

Details
Share where collusion is chosen (left chart)

Raising the preferenced volume share from 0 to 0.6 raises the share of cells where collusion is chosen from 0.26 to 0.33. Why: preferenced flow is shared whatever the price, so an undercut steals less volume and cheating pays less.

Raising the tip probability from 0 to 0.05 lowers the share of cells where collusion is chosen from 0.38 to 0.20. Why: tips end cartels at any margin, so they shorten the expected life of the cartel.

Highest: 0.42 at φ = 0.6, ρ_tip = 0; lowest: 0.17 at φ = 0, ρ_tip = 0.05.

Mechanism detail: Nearly additive: φ adds about 0.05–0.07 at every tip level (no tip: 0.351 → 0.421; 5%: 0.171 → 0.225), while the 5% tip roughly halves the share. The tip also lowers effective patience in the incentive constraint and φ is the counterweight: the share of cells where collusion is sustainable at φ = 0 falls from 0.690 to 0.656 with a 5% tip, at φ = 0.6 only from 0.860 to 0.836.

Chosen collusion intensity θ* (right chart)

Raising the preferenced volume share from 0 to 0.6 raises the mean collusion intensity θ* from 0.17 to 0.24. Why: preferenced flow is shared whatever the price, so an undercut steals less volume and cheating pays less.

Raising the tip probability from 0 to 0.05 lowers the mean collusion intensity θ* from 0.25 to 0.14. Why: tips end cartels at any margin, so they shorten the expected life of the cartel.

Highest: 0.29 at φ = 0.6, ρ_tip = 0; lowest: 0.12 at φ = 0, ρ_tip = 0.05.

Mechanism detail: θ* falls with the tip at every φ (φ = 0: 0.222 → 0.118) through participation, even though cartels that still collude choose higher margins under a tip (see the δ × ρ_tip and slope × ρ_tip splits). φ adds 0.05–0.07 to θ* at every tip level.
