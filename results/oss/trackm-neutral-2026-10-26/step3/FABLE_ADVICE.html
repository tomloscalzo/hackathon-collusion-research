Consultation 1 (about 19:45 ET, on partial data: 435 of 720 games, biased to short games)

Advice, summarised.

  1. Lead with the payoff block. On provisional numbers (the advisor's own pass over the DONE games of the corrected formula / no-numbers rerun, no bootstrap), every corrected cell is below its headline cell on the price index: no numbers tacit p1 -0.38 (19 of 20 pairs negative), formula tacit p1 -0.21, no numbers chat p1 -0.57 (9 of 9), formula chat p1 -0.35, formula chat p5 -0.35. The ordering matches Qwen (no numbers lowest; formula plus chat keeps a residual negotiated price). Frame anchoring as "the printed numbers set the price level", not "the model copies a number": only about 28% of gpt-oss quotes sit exactly on a printed value, but about 32% sit one to three ticks under one (2.49, 2.48, 2.47). Report the within-one-tick share.
  2. Headline (both models, same sign, n >= 10 pairs in the final build): the payoff-block result (1.1, 2.1); private chat raises collusion, and more for gpt-oss because its tacit baseline is far more competitive while its chat cell reaches its own joint-profit control (1.1); the lever-transfer table under the pre-declared same-sign rule (moral prime B vs placebo, 5-tick start, planted cheater, heavy fine, CoT threat A; controls calibrate both) (1.5); the CoT threat works by suppression, not a shift to other channels, on both models (3.4).
  3. Supporting: watchdog ask-and-report vs ask-no-report (suggestive, pending final n); incentives, asymmetric rules, 3 vs 2 firms, introspection and self-score arms as bounded nulls.
  4. Dropped or demoted: any finding with fewer than 10 pairs regardless of its q (three firms with one ethics rule; R4 announced); every thinking-mode claim ("not addressed"); anchored run and monitored chat (not run); say-versus-do, intent-class and candour numbers as claims (keyword rules unvalidated on gpt-oss text).
  5. Ad-hoc priorities: (1) focal points with fx-ctl-joint as the capability test, the exact and within-one-tick shares, and quote histograms; (2) the compare-models lever table; (3) detection transfer: apply the Qwen-frozen detector to gpt-oss games (2.2, 2.3; AI-control "does the monitor transfer"); (4) CoT threat channel shift plus a reasoning read; (5) same-prompt reproducibility from the hrep-* replicate cells (2.3); (6) the remaining topics on the template, with no extra review hours on the small-n topics.
  6. Framing traps: medium effort is not "thinking on"; quote no magnitudes from partial data; require n >= 10 to call anything a finding; magnitude differences are confounded with the baseline level (gpt-oss tacit 0.39 vs Qwen 0.72), so report both levels; a low exact-on-printed share is not "no anchoring"; keep the same-sign rule and never pool models; name empty_talk_as_done as an engine difference; the strict-compete control is 0.01 on gpt-oss, not 0.00.
  7. The story, in order: (a) printed numbers set the level in both models, so default collusion is a property of the prompt design, not a model trait; (b) gpt-oss is far more competitive without chat but chat takes it to its joint-profit level; (c) the lever set transfers in sign, with the same suppression pattern under the idle monitor; (d) the self-report and introspection instruments are uninformative on both models.
