Rule: these seven comparisons are the only ones that carry claim weight. Each has one primary contrast with a stated sign convention, read on the run-level statistic of spec §5.1, P(adhere): the share of rounds 1 … min(8, L) (L the realised length) that precede the partner's first punishment phase in which the LLM's own quote is at or above the grid-rounded s(θ_p) = 2.09; every run counts and none is excluded on its length; the mean own-quote CI and P(collude) (own-quote CI ≥ 0.1) over the same rounds are secondary and descriptive; unless stated. Each contrast is reported with a one-sided bootstrap confidence interval clustered on paraphrase, its paraphrase spread (range and SD across the ≥ 10 paraphrases), and the scripted reference bands (tit-for-tat, grim, noise seats) computed at the same design size (spec §5.8). Everything else observed is exploratory and will be labelled so.

Outcomes (fixed now, spec §5.4). For each prediction: SUPPORTED if the Holm-adjusted one-sided lower confidence bound of the primary contrast exceeds the upper edge of the reference band of the seats named in the Notes column (a point for a deterministic seat, so the LLM contrast must then exceed the imitator's contrast with its adjusted interval; zero where no band applies); REFUTED if either the point estimate has the wrong sign and the one-sided interval for the opposite direction excludes zero, or the Holm-adjusted one-sided upper confidence bound of the contrast is below 0.1 in the statistic's units (a precise null refutes); the SUPPORTED test is decided first and REFUTED is checked only when it fails (an interval lying entirely between the band edge and 0.1 is SUPPORTED, with its small size reported); INCONCLUSIVE otherwise, and always when a cell has fewer than 30 analysable runs (after the refusal exclusion and, for P3, the inclusion and termination rules; vignette: 30 analysable calls). The design therefore starts 10 paraphrases × 4 seeds = 40 runs per game cell, and P3 probe and control cells add seeds in order until 30 included runs (a stopping rule on the count only, never on outcomes). Game refusal = an explicit decline or no parseable quote after the repair retry; an immediate deviation is a decision, not a refusal. The Holm-adjusted one-sided interval for a test at Holm step-down level α_i is the one-sided (1 − α_i) percentile interval of the paraphrase-cluster bootstrap (B = 4000). Holm is applied per model over the fixed family {P1-A, P3, P4, P5, P6a, P6b, P6c, P6d, P7} (9 tests; P2 is a harness check and P1-A′ a sanity control, both outside the family; tests whose variant is not built count as not rejected). Contrasts are paired: the horizon stream and the demand-noise streams are shared across the arms of a prediction within each (paraphrase, seed) cell. Refusal and parse failures are reported per model and condition; refusers are excluded from the numeric contrasts (guard 3), and because that is a post-treatment selection each contrast is shown with the refusal rate per arm and, when the arms' refusal rates differ by more than 10 points, with a bound computed with refusers scored at CI = 0 (vignette: competitive choice). The analysis script tools/analysis.py is frozen (hash in the manifest) before the first LLM call.

Revised 2026-10-23, before any LLM call: refusal operationalisation. No predicted direction, contrast, arm, threshold or outcome rule is changed. These are definition changes: each one can change which runs or calls are analysable, and so a cell's outcome, and the reason is given with each. Where they differ from the refusal sentences of the Outcomes paragraph above, they replace them. (i) What counts as a refusal. A game round is a refusal when the model declines in its JSON reply (a null quote or a true decline), declines in prose without a JSON object (the refusal pattern of INTERFACES.md e.4, which also matches typographic apostrophes), declines through a provider refusal field (counted as an explicit decline), is stopped by a provider content or prompt filter (a "provider block": excluded and bounded like a refusal, but reported separately and never counted as a model refusal; a filtered reply whose text is still complete is read as an ordinary reply), or leaves no parseable quote after the one repair retry (reported separately when the last reply was cut off at the token limit). Reason: without this, provider refusals and filter blocks are silently missing runs, and unparseable replies were imputed and kept, against the declared definition. (ii) Truncated replies. A reply cut off at the token limit whose complete leading quote can be read is read as that quote, with no repair. Reason: a complete quote token is a parseable quote. (iii) After the first refusal. The model is not called again in that run; the seat quotes s₀ for the rest of the run, and the refusal round and every later round are excluded from the statistics. Reason: no fabricated history is shown to the model, and the tail equals the score the bound gives a refuser (CI = 0). (iv) Which runs are refusers. A game run is a refuser (excluded from the primary contrasts, scored in the bound, counted in the refusal rate) only when its first refusal falls inside the statistic's window: rounds 1 … min(8, L) before the partner's first punishment phase; for P3 probe and control runs, rounds 1 … t + 2 (t the defection round, or the placebo round of the control; a control partner without one uses the statistic window). A later refusal does not exclude the run; it is reported in an any-round refusal rate. Reason: exclusion for refusals after the window would grow with the realised length, against "none is excluded on its length". (v) Vignette. A refused row (NEITHER or a decline, a provider refusal or a provider block) is dropped from that pass's b̂ only; one refused row does not exclude the pass (the unit is the call, spec §5.4). A pass is a refuser only when none of its rows is a valid choice and at least one is refused. A row still unreadable after the repair stays invalid (excluded and counted, not a refusal). A pass that is always competitive, i.e. whose switch point lies below the lowest offered payment, enters P6c/P6d at that payment, −0.5 G (also when the −0.5 row itself has no valid call) (left-censored, which understates the label and guardrail effects), and its share is reported per arm as the always-competitive refusal pattern. "30 analysable calls" counts the valid choices of non-refuser passes. A vignette arm's refusal rate is its share of refused calls, and the pre-declared bound scores every refused call as the competitive choice. Reason: the call is the unit, and dropping a whole pass for one refused row would discard its observed choices. (vi) P2 needs 30 non-refusing runs in each control arm, and the E2 and competitive arms' refusal rates are printed beside the verdict. Reason: a model that mostly cannot quote under E2 could otherwise pass on its few surviving runs. (vii) Worst-case bracket. Besides the pre-declared bound, a worst-case bracket is always shown: its lower end scores arm A's refusers at 0 and arm B's at 1 (vignette: arm A's refused calls competitive, arm B's collusive), its upper end the reverse. It is descriptive and does not enter the outcome rule.

Environment constants held across the arms of every prediction (spec §5.1): the reacting partner quotes s(θ_p) with θ_p = 0.546 (the ripe baseline's θ*), trigger tolerance 0 (any grid quote below s(θ_p) triggers), k = ∞ (grim: s₀ for the rest of the game), rule disclosed in plain words, continuation probability 0.95 disclosed, cap 40 undisclosed, audit probability and penalty disclosed.

What would count against the model as a whole: P1-A refuted for a model that passes P2 (the ripeness index has no directional content for LLM behaviour); P3 refuted across models (LLM "collusion" is not supported by conditional strategies, so Layer A's mechanism is the wrong description); P5 refuted (a disclosed audit probability that makes adhering unsustainable but still myopically profitable does not move adherence, so the continuation term of the map has no directional content for the models tested). These are statements about the models tested, not about LLMs in general. Inconclusive outcomes count neither way and are reported as such.
