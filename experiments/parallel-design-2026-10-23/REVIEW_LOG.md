One fresh adversarial reviewer attacked the plan at about 14:45 ET on feasibility, cost, statistical validity and relevance, after all five planning documents, the design CSVs, the scripts and the proposed scenario files existed. It read the plan against the pre-declared predictions, the spec, the interface documents, the engine and prompt code, and the areas of interest. Seventeen findings: seven MATERIAL, ten NOT MATERIAL. ## Converging or diverging?

The material findings **converge on one layer**: the pre-declaration surface was not one frozen artefact. Prose, CSV, generated JSON, the seed plan, the keyword rule and placeholder signs disagreed (findings 2, 3, 4, 7), and the guard against over-claiming checked numbers rather than claims (5). The economics and the pre-declared statistics were checked against the engine and prompt code and hold (4, 9, 10). A smaller cluster is the clock (1, 13, 16): honest when written, already behind. No finding touched the model, the spec or the predictions file, and none suggested a fundamental flaw in the design; the lead reads this as a converging review with no sign of incorrigibility. The fix is a single freezing commit, now specified in `EXPLORATORY_PREDECLARATION_DRAFT.md` §5.
